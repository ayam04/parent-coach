Safety and limits

The coach does not diagnose, and it does not give medical or legal instructions.

If you describe an emergency, abuse, or self-harm, it puts safety steps first and urges you to contact local emergency or crisis services straight away, for example 112 or 911.

Your privacy

Your conversation is saved to an anonymous session ID in this browser, so you can pick it up later. There is no login. Avoid sharing names, addresses, or other identifying details.
